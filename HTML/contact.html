A note for contributors

Bring us something you cannot stop noticing.

The strongest Kahel stories begin with a specific place, person, object, ritual, or question—and open outward from there.

Pitch a story
  1. 01

    Introduce the idea

    In a few sentences, tell us what you want to explore and why it matters now.

  2. 02

    Show us your angle

    Share the people, places, access, or lived experience that will make the story yours.

  3. 03

    Include useful links

    Add two or three relevant samples. Rough work and personal projects are welcome.

  4. 04

    Give it a little time

    We read every considered pitch and aim to respond within two working weeks.

Before you write

A few useful answers.

Do you accept unsolicited contributions?

Yes. A concise proposal is usually more useful than a finished piece because it gives us room to shape the story together.

Do contributors need to be based in the Philippines?

No. Kahel is Filipino in inspiration and open in outlook. We welcome thoughtful work rooted in many places and communities.

Can organizations propose partnerships?

Yes, when the collaboration respects editorial independence and creates something genuinely useful or meaningful for readers.

Can I ask for help using the Kahel theme?

Absolutely. Include your WordPress version, a link or screenshot, and a clear description of what you expected to happen.
